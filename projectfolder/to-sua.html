Stop 01 · Lotofaga

To Sua Ocean Trench

The first time you see the water, it almost doesn’t look real. To Sua is a deep natural swimming hole on Upolu’s south coast, reached by a steep ladder through a garden.

Take your time here

There’s more to this stop than the swim. The grounds open toward the coast, where lava rock, tropical plants, and ocean views make it easy to slow down between dips. I’d bring water, leave a little room in the day, and enjoy the view before heading back up.

Good to know

Where: Lotofaga, south-east Upolu

Bring: Swimwear, a towel, and shoes with grip

Keep in mind: The ladder is steep. Check conditions and follow the site’s safety instructions.
