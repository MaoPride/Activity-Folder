Stop 4 • Savai'i

Alofaaga Blowholes

At Alofaaga, waves push seawater through openings in volcanic rock and send it high into the air. The blowholes show the powerful forces that have shaped Samoa's islands. The rocks can be slippery and the waves unpredictable, so visitors should stay well back from the openings and follow local advice.
