Stop 5 • Savai'i

Saleaula Lava Fields

The Saleaula Lava Fields show the lasting impact of volcanic activity on Savai'i. Lava from the Mount Matavanu eruption in the early 1900s covered parts of the area, including the remains of a church. This place combines striking scenery with an important reminder of Samoa's natural history.
