Stop 3 • Upolu

Papaseea Sliding Rocks

Papaseea Sliding Rocks is a scenic spot in Samoa where water flows over smooth, dark volcanic rocks surrounded by lush tropical vegetation. The area offers a peaceful retreat for visitors seeking to experience the natural beauty of the island's interior.
