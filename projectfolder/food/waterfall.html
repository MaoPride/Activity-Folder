Stop 3 • Upolu

Papapapaitai Falls

Papapapaitai Falls drops into a deep, green valley on Upolu. The waterfall can be seen from a roadside lookout, making it a memorable stop for visitors exploring the island's interior. The surrounding rainforest shows another side of Samoa beyond its beaches.
