Stop 2 • Upolu

To Sua Ocean Trench

To Sua is a famous natural swimming hole surrounded by lush tropical plants. A ladder leads down toward the water, making the view from above especially memorable. Visitors should check local conditions and follow all safety instructions before entering the water.
