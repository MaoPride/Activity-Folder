Stop 1 • Upolu

Lalomanu Beach

Lalomanu Beach is known for its pale sand, clear lagoon, and views of small islands offshore. Traditional beach fales give the coastline a distinct Samoan character. It is a lovely place to appreciate the Pacific Ocean and enjoy the scenery.
