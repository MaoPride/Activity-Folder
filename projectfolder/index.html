My Whole Family

This is a picture of my whole family. We are all very close and support each other in everything we do. Even though we get on each other's nerves sometimes, we are always there for each other.

We always make time for each other, whether it's for a family dinner, a holiday gathering, or just a simple phone call. Our family is my biggest source of inspiration and support. Being far away from them can be difficult, but I feel their support from Samoa. There is a saying in Samoa: "O le ala i le pule o le tautua," which means "The way to power is through service." This reminds me that our family's strength comes from how we support and serve each other.
