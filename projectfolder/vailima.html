Stop 05 · Vailima

Robert Louis Stevenson Museum

Set in the old Vailima home, this museum tells the story of the Scottish writer who spent his final years in Samoa and became known locally as Tusitala, “teller of tales.”

More than a beautiful house

The rooms and grounds offer a glimpse into a particular chapter of Samoa’s history. A visit is a chance to learn about Stevenson’s life and also to think about how Samoa’s own people and stories shaped the place he called home. If time allows, the nearby Mount Vaea trail leads toward his resting place.

Make it a visit

Where: Vailima, just outside Apia

Look for: Museum opening hours and guided tour details

Be ready: For a bit of walking around the grounds.
