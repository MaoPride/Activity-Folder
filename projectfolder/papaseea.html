Stop 03 · Near Apia

Papase’ea Sliding Rocks

A short drive from Apia, Papase’ea brings you into a green pocket of the island where fresh water runs over smooth rock into natural pools.

Fresh water, forest shade

The rocks are famous for their natural slides, but the setting is just as memorable: tropical growth, small cascades, and the sound of water all around. Slippery surfaces can be risky, so take your time and only slide where local signs or guides say it’s safe.

Before you go

Wear: A swimsuit and sturdy water shoes

Check: Water levels and weather before entering

Respect: Keep the area clean and follow posted instructions.
