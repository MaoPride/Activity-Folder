Stop 02 · East Upolu

Lalomanu Beach

Lalomanu is one of those places where the best plan is to make no plan at all: swim in the lagoon, sit in the shade, and watch the light change over Nu’utele Island.

A slower beach day

The coastline is known for clear water and a reef-protected lagoon. Beach fales make the shoreline feel welcoming and close to village life. I’d keep the day simple and ask locally about swimming areas, reef conditions, and any village customs to follow.

Small reminders

Bring: Reef-safe sun protection, water, and cash for local purchases

Be thoughtful: Ask before taking pictures of people or private spaces.

Leave gently: Take rubbish with you and avoid standing on coral.
