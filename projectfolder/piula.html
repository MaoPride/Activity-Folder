Stop 04 · North coast

Piula Cave Pool

Piula is a freshwater pool beside the sea, tucked beneath the grounds of Piula Theological College. The water stays cool, and the cave gives the swim a little sense of discovery.

A cool-down stop

This is a quieter kind of outing: descend to the pool, take a swim, and enjoy the contrast of fresh water and ocean nearby. Because it sits beside a college, it’s especially important to dress modestly while walking through the grounds and to respect any visiting hours or local requests.

Plan kindly

Where: Along Upolu’s north coast

Bring: A towel and modest cover-up

Remember: Check access details with local hosts before visiting.
